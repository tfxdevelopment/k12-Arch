# CAF-01: Strategy - Business Outcomes and Cloud Rationale

## Metadata
- **Status:** Draft
- **Framework:** Microsoft Cloud Adoption Framework - Strategy
- **Related:** [CAF-02 Plan](CAF-02-plan.md), [EXECUTIVE-BRIEF](../EXECUTIVE-BRIEF.md)

## Strategic Drivers
- Support sustained enrollment growth without seasonal service degradation.
- Improve delivery speed for policy and eligibility rule changes.
- Strengthen compliance posture while reducing operational risk.
- Enable analytics-led decisions for student outcomes and program operations.

## Business Outcomes
| Outcome | KPI | Target |
|---|---|---:|
| Better enrollment experience | Successful submission rate | > 99.5% |
| Faster feature delivery | Lead time for change | < 7 days for standard changes |
| Higher platform resilience | Enrollment-season uptime | 99.95% |
| Better cost transparency | Unit economics visibility | Cost/applicant tracked monthly |

## Constraints and Assumptions
- Existing identity and access model (Entra-based) remains foundational.
- Migration must be incremental with clear rollback points.
- Compliance obligations cannot regress during modernization.

## Strategy Checklist
- [ ] Confirm measurable success metrics with product and operations leadership
- [ ] Align modernization scope with fiscal-year budget cycle
- [ ] Define executive reporting cadence for migration progress and risk
